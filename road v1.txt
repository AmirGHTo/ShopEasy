### The Full Production Checklist

**Observability (you have tracing, missing the rest)**
```
✅ Distributed tracing (Jaeger)
⬜ Centralized logging (ELK / Loki + Grafana)
⬜ Metrics (Prometheus + Grafana dashboards)
⬜ Alerting (PagerDuty / OpsGenie)
```
Tracing alone is not enough. Logs tell you what happened. Metrics tell you trends. Traces tell you where time was spent. You need all three — this is called the **observability triangle**.

---

**Resilience (you have basic Polly, missing the rest)**
```
✅ Retry with exponential backoff (Polly)
✅ Circuit breaker (Polly)
⬜ Rate limiting (protect services from being overwhelmed)
⬜ Bulkhead isolation (failures in one service don't exhaust resources in another)
⬜ Timeout policies (every external call must have a deadline)
⬜ Fallback strategies (graceful degradation when dependencies fail)
```

## My Honest Recommendation

**Don't implement Istio yet.** It's a massive operational overhead — it adds a sidecar proxy to every pod, requires deep Kubernetes knowledge, and is overkill unless you have 20+ services and a dedicated platform team. Most companies with 5-10 services don't need it.

**Message schema registry (Avro/Protobuf)** is important conceptually but adds significant complexity. In practice, most teams use JSON with careful versioning conventions rather than a full schema registry unless they have dozens of services and teams.
