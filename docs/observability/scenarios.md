# Failure scenarios: the demo script

Each scenario causes a failure the alerts are written for, and says what happens: which alert fires (in order of appearance), which dashboard panel moves, which log or trace explains it, how it recovers. It is also the script of the live demo in [presentation.md](presentation.md). Use it with traffic: `ops/demo/load.sh --rate 20 --duration 900`. `ops/demo/break.sh <scenario>` causes the failure and prints what to expect and where to look; `ops/demo/fix.sh <scenario>` undoes it. (The six scenarios are those of `SCENARIOS` in [`ops/demo/lib.sh`](../../ops/demo/lib.sh).)

Where to look: alerts http://localhost:9090/alerts, Alertmanager http://localhost:9093, mails http://localhost:8025, Grafana http://localhost:3000, Jaeger http://localhost:16686, Seq http://localhost:5341. The alerts and their `for:` times are those of [`prometheus-rules.yml`](../../prometheus-rules.yml); the runbooks are in the [README](../../README.md#runbooks).

| Scenario | The alerts, in order of appearance | The panel that moves | What explains it | How it recovers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `payment` (Payment stopped) | `ServiceDown` (1 min); `QueueWithoutConsumers` for `payment-process` (2 min; shown as inhibited in Alertmanager, because `ServiceDown` explains it) | Service Health: the payment instance is DOWN. Order Flow: orders stop at "stock reserved"; the oldest order in progress rises | Jaeger: an order placed now stops after the reservation. Seq: `OrderId = '<id>'` | `fix.sh payment` starts it; the waiting payments are processed and the queue drains. An order that waited past the saga timeout (about two minutes) was cancelled and its stock released; its late payment is refunded ([Testing failure scenarios](../../README.md#testing-failure-scenarios)) |
| `postgres` (PostgreSQL stopped) | `DependencyDown` for `postgres` (1 min); `ShopServerErrorsHigh` follows and is held back | Dependencies: connections fall to 0. Service Health: the error ratio rises | Seq: `@Level = 'Error'` (connection refused); each service's `/health/ready` fails | `fix.sh postgres`: the services reconnect on their own, because the pool drops dead connections ([Surviving database and network outages](../../README.md#24-surviving-database-and-network-outages)) |
| `rabbitmq` (the broker stopped) | `DependencyDown` for `rabbitmq` (1 min); with load `SagaCommandsNotSent` (2 min), `OutboxBacklog` (5 min), `PollingLoopStalled` (all three fire; their mails are held back by `DependencyDown`) | Order Flow: the outbox and saga-command backlogs grow, no order moves on | Seq: the broker is logged as not ready, once | `fix.sh rabbitmq`: the outbox delivers what waited, the backlog drains, the orders finish. Nothing was lost; the broker's queues are on a volume |
| `order` (the Order service killed) | `ServiceDown` (1 min); with load `ShopServerErrorsHigh` (held back) | Service Health: the order instance is DOWN, its requests fall to 0 | Seq: the gateway's failed calls to order | `fix.sh order`: the sagas continue from where the database says they were |
| `product-hang` (Product frozen with `docker pause`) | `ServiceDown` (its metrics time out, 1 min); with load `ShopServerErrorsHigh` (5 min) and `ShopSlow` (10 min), both held back by `ServiceDown` | Service Health: latency p95 climbs to the gateway's 30 s timeout; "5xx by endpoint" shows the product routes | Jaeger: the slowest traces of the gateway; the product call is the whole request | `fix.sh product-hang` unfreezes it; the percentile recovers as its window slides |
| `poison` (a payment command no one can process) | `DeadLettersPresent` for `payment-process_error` (5 min, critical) | Order Flow: the dead letters panel shows 1 | The Payment log: `null value in column "UserId" ... violates not-null constraint` (SqlState 23502), then the move to the error queue; the Dead Letter Monitor lists it | `fix.sh poison` empties the error queue (this message has no cause to fix; a real dead letter is requeued from `/deadletters` once its cause is fixed) |

| *not a script:* `docker compose stop product` (with load) | `ServiceDown` (1 min), then `ErrorBudgetFastBurn` (pending after about 2 minutes, firing after 3 to 4; critical); `ErrorBudgetSlowBurn` too if it lasts a quarter of an hour. Neither is held back | SLOs: the burn rate over 5 minutes at once, over an hour within minutes | Service Health: 5xx by endpoint; Seq: the gateway's failed calls to product | `docker compose start product`: the fast alert resolves about 5 minutes later, while the hour behind it is still high ([Service level objectives](../../README.md#service-level-objectives)) |

`product-hang` freezes Product instead of slowing it: a throttled container is slow by an amount that depends on the machine, a frozen one is the same everywhere. The README says which of these were watched happen on a real Prometheus and which are read from the rules ([Traffic and failures to try](../../README.md#traffic-and-failures-to-try)).
